Auto-post trending news
Every 3 hours the server collects stories from the sources saved in your Supabase database (table news_sources: Sky Sports, Fox Sports, ESPN, SuperSport, Global Publishers, BBC and targeted searches for Tanzanian clubs, Miss Grand and Bongo Flava artists), and saves each one as an article with its photo, a short teaser and a clear source credit + link. A story whose source link is already in the database is never added twice. This runs on the server, so no keys or tokens are ever stored in this page.
Social posting: one designed 1080×1350 card (photo, category, Swahili headline and teaser) is posted automatically to Instagram and Facebook every hour. Stories about Simba, Yanga, Azam, Miss Grand Tanzania and the Bongo Flava stars (Diamond, Harmonize, Rayvanny, Alikiba) go first. Stories not posted within 24 hours are dropped. Progress and errors are recorded per post in the social_posts table, and access tokens live in the private social_credentials table, never in this page.
