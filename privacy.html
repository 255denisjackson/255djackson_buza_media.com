1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Buza Media ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Buza Media website and related services (the "Service"). It also describes the choices available to you regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two general ways: information you provide to us directly, and information collected automatically when you use the Service. Each is described below.

3. Information You Provide

Depending on which features of the Service you use, you may choose to provide information such as your name and email address — for example, if you subscribe to updates, contact us, or leave a comment on an article. We only collect what is reasonably needed to provide the relevant feature.

4. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Service, certain information may be collected automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring website. This information helps us operate, secure, and improve the Service.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand how visitors use the Service, and, where applicable, support advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some features of the Service.

6. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Respond to inquiries and provide support
  • Send updates you have requested, such as newsletters
  • Understand how the Service is used, through analytics
  • Protect the security and integrity of the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

7. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to help us understand how visitors interact with the Service, such as which articles are read and how visitors navigate the site. Analytics data is generally used in aggregate to inform editorial and product decisions.

8. Advertising

If Buza Media displays advertising, our advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to help deliver and measure ads. Where this occurs, it is subject to the applicable partner's own privacy practices. We do not sell the personal information we collect directly through the Service.

9. Social Media Platforms

Buza Media maintains profiles on social media platforms and may embed social media content (such as posts or videos) on our website. If you interact with our content on a social media platform, that interaction is also governed by the privacy policy of that platform.

10. Third-Party Services

We may work with third-party service providers to help operate the Service, such as hosting, analytics, or email delivery providers. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are expected to handle it responsibly.

11. API Integrations

Where Buza Media integrates with third-party APIs — for example, to publish content, retrieve public data, or enable a feature on our site — the integration accesses only the information necessary for that feature to work, and operates in accordance with the relevant platform's developer policies as well as this Privacy Policy.

12. TikTok Integration

Buza Media may integrate with TikTok's developer platform — for example, to allow content to be shared to TikTok, to display TikTok content on our website, or to authenticate a TikTok-related feature. Where such integration is used, it accesses only the information necessary to provide that specific feature, in accordance with TikTok's developer terms and policies. We do not use TikTok's APIs to collect information beyond what is needed for the integration to function, and any information obtained through a TikTok integration is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the Service, when required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

14. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

16. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please reach out through the contact channels published on the Buza Media website.

17. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

18. International Data Transfers

Buza Media operates from the United Republic of Tanzania. If you access the Service from outside Tanzania, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Tanzania or in other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

20. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle information, please reach out through the contact channels published on the Buza Media website.
